# Evidence Press method alignment

Current context checked on 1 September 2026 against the public Evidence Press
operating model and method registry updated 31 August 2026.

## Methods illustrated

| Registry method | This candidate's use | Preserved failure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `structural-compression` | Replaces an unconstrained search over infinite-type groups by one fixed normal subgroup of a compact surface group and one scalar pinching parameter | The family is proved to give Fuchsian points in `T_qc^red(X0)`; no claim that it parametrizes all of that space |
| `adversarial-controls` | Mutates genus, deck rank, collar counts, seam trace, and length domain | Producer-designed controls are not independent confirmation and do not verify the theorem |
| `assurance-vector` | Separates written proof, cited theorem dependence, finite replay, novelty search, external review, formal verification, and publication | No scalar badge or internal pass promotes an unassessed dimension |
| `agent-readable-research-object` | Supplies claim, proof-obligation, source, novelty, assurance, and replay files | Machine-readable consistency is not mathematical truth |

## Decision object

A complete internally reviewed all-Fuchsian strengthening candidate with an explicit construction and the
one-sided quantitative bound `1-delta=O_g(ell)`. The next high-value assurance step is
an unaffiliated Fuchsian-group specialist review or independent proof
reconstruction, not more finite sampling.

## Clock boundary

The archived research metrics measure the research-only solve attempt.
Discovery, assurance, publication, and translation clocks remain separate.
The publication attempt is measured prospectively in the Evidence Press work
ledger rather than retrofitted into the research receipt.

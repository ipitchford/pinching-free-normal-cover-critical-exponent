# Provenance

The target was selected during an Evidence Press screening of the UnsolvedMath
archive and traced to M. Kapovich's Problem 4.3 in the AIM *Problems on
Thurston Metric* list. The present task began after the user instructed Codex
to solve AIM-TOPOLOGY-0203 in full.

OpenAI Codex developed the free-normal-cover construction, performed the
primary-source and bounded novelty searches, wrote and tested the proof,
produced the LaTeX/Markdown/PDF manuscript, built the finite formula checks,
and assembled the original research package under user direction. A supplied
full review dated 1 September 2026 recommended minor revisions. The original
attachment remains privately available; because redistribution authority was
not established, the public successor retains only its SHA-256 and a
producer-authored response. Codex actioned its repair roadmap in this successor
package and prepared the publication records. Scholarly attribution
is anonymous. The cited mathematical results retain their original
authorship.

No source PDF is redistributed. `SOURCES.md` records the hashes of the exact
primary PDFs inspected. Generated replay code checks only package-original
formulas and metadata.

Ian Pitchford is the repository maintainer and Evidence Press publisher, not a
scholarly creator. Internal model-mediated editorial reports, when present,
are publication-workflow evidence and do not constitute unaffiliated review.
Current public identifiers and deployment receipts are recorded in `STATUS.md`
and `PUBLICATION_RECEIPT.json`; their existence does not alter assurance.

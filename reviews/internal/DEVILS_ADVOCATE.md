**Frozen archive:** `aim-topology-0203-v0.2.0-pre-editorial.zip`  
**SHA-256:** `9754197b7185f26f54910379787bb53aba630bfc6c6d6e29c05c20cbf5154ae1`  
**Decision:** Major Revision

## Critical findings

The archive does not yet prove that its surface-based reduced space supplies
points in the group-theoretic `T_qc(Gamma_0)` appearing in the exact AIM
problem. The exact problem must be quoted and a load-bearing proposition or
lemma must construct sphere conjugacies, compare the relevant reduced
equivalence, and establish exponent invariance. Domain confirmation is
required after repair.

Third-party redistribution and font rights are unresolved. The raw supplied
review has `NOASSERTION` rights yet appears in the intended CC0 public archive.
It must be removed unless redistribution authority is documented. The PDF
embeds Apple Songti subsets although the rights map says fonts are not
redistributed; an auditable redistributable font and corrected rights map are
required.

## Major findings

The PDF build uses an absolute TinyTeX path and system font; CI validates but
does not rebuild the PDF, and marker-only parity is too weak. The build must be
portable, use redistributable fonts, run in CI, and bind the complete normalized
rendered text. Assurance and “target closed” metadata must be reconciled.
Dougall--Sharp and Sullivan applications need exact statement locations and
hypothesis-by-hypothesis maps.

## Minor findings

The broad novelty risk remains substantial; no “new,” “first,” or unqualified
“solution” language is supported. Research metrics must be version-bound.
Structured creator identity should be normalized. The untagged PDF limitation
must be disclosed.

The core construction appears coherent and repairable. The archive replayed
successfully with 42 samples and 14 tests, but acceptance of this frozen object
is impermissible until the semantic, rights, build, assurance, and citation
repairs are confirmed. No conflict was identified; this was internal
archive-only review, not specialist review or peer review.

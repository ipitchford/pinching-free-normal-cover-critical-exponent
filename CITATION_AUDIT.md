## Applicability boundary

The package does not re-prove imported theorems. Producer inspection found the
needed hypotheses: the ambient group is cocompact and convex cocompact; the
subgroup is normal; the quotient is the nonamenable group `F_g`; and the
constructed group is nonelementary. The spectral formula is used only after
the direct Rayleigh bound makes `lambda_0<1/4`.

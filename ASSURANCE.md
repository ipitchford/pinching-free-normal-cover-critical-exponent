# Assurance statement

## Supported by this package

- A complete written construction for every genus `g >= 2`.
- A proof that the constructed groups are infinitely generated and of the
  first kind.
- A definition and proof that the marked covers give Fuchsian points in one
  reduced, surface-based quasiconformal Teichmuller space.
- A proof that every marked compact-base exponent is strictly below one, conditional
  on the cited Dougall--Sharp theorem.
- An explicit compactly supported test function and exact collar-energy and
  core-area calculations.
- The quantitative bound `1 - delta = O_g(ell)` and the conclusion `delta -> 1`,
  conditional on the cited Elstrodt--Patterson--Sullivan formula.
- Standard-library finite replay of the explicit analytic formulas and
  mutation tests guarding the main factors and domain restrictions.
- A source ledger and bounded novelty search current to 1 September 2026.
- Internal model-mediated editorial review: five-role substantive round,
  material repairs, and one exact-archive domain confirmation, with final
  `PASS_WITH_NOTES`. This is not unaffiliated specialist review, independent
  reconstruction, or peer review.

## Not supported

- Exhaustive historical novelty or priority.
- Independent reconstruction by an unaffiliated mathematician.
- Authentication or endorsement by M. Kapovich, AIM, or any cited author.
- Formal proof-assistant verification.
- Conventional external peer review or journal acceptance.
- Publication, DOI assignment, GitHub release, or Evidence Press deployment.

The internal calculations are producer-side corroboration.  They do not
verify the cited theorems, the semantic correctness of the model, or the
literature search.

The supplied review is a workflow input; its raw third-party text is excluded
from the public package and represented there by a hash and producer response.
It is not authenticated as specialist review. The PDF is untagged. `paper.md`
is an accessible-text fallback with linearized plain-text mathematics, not a
claim of full semantic-math accessibility.
